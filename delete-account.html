Delete Your SIAS Account

This page explains how users of the SIAS app, developed by FrameBig, can request that their account and associated data be deleted.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with your SIAS account to:
    support@framebig.com.bd
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my SIAS account".
  3. Include your username and company name in the email.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you are the account owner.
  5. Your account will be deleted within 30 days of your verified request, and you will receive a confirmation email.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your user account, profile information and login credentials
  • Company, branch and user information linked to your account
  • Product, category, stock, customer, supplier and sales data stored on our servers
  • Uploaded images (company logo, product and category images)

Data that may be retained

  • Payment and subscription records may be kept for up to 5 years where required by tax or accounting laws, after which they are permanently deleted.
  • Server backups containing your data are automatically overwritten within 90 days.

Questions

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data, contact us at support@framebig.com.bd or visit framebig.com.bd.
